We had a fabulous time participating in the IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), September 27 through October 1, 2026.
We presented one paper in the contributed talks and three workshop papers in the [Space Robotics Workshop](https://space-robots.org/events/iros2026/){:target="\_blank"}.
We also visited and toured Astrobotic and the CMU Robotics Innovation Center (RIC) in Pittsburgh! :)
